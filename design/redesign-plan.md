# Orchha Palace: mobile-first redesign plan

Implementation status (14 September 2026): the approved features, the owner's 11 annotated revisions, the suggested-stay usability revision, and the second editorial review are implemented and deployed. Nine logos remain included, with SBI third; IRIA and Chemists & Druggists Federation are excluded, and ASISC is omitted because its artwork could not be retrieved reliably. The redesigned site was deployed to orchhapalace.com on 14 September 2026. See `../design-qa.md` for current evidence.

The annotated revisions supersede the compact, booking-first hero and three-category homepage specified below. The final homepage and wedding page use immersive photograph backdrops, with reservations below the homepage hero and a single spacious rooms-and-suites editorial section leading to the full room chooser. The supplied hotel emblem is used for favicon and touch icons. The one-night FAQ is removed without restricting one-night searches.

Wedding gallery order is daytime garden celebration, fireworks/reception, indoor floral mandap, amphitheatre celebration, celebration dining, then private date. Its optional complete-image presentation uses a stable 3:2 frame below 960px and 16:9 above, with object-fit contain and a neutral background. Room-gallery defaults remain unchanged. Thumbnail navigation scrolls only the strip; decoded image, caption and selection commit together. Corporate gallery order is boardroom, outdoor gathering, then celebration dining. Venue finders start with Any layout and all six wedding or seven corporate spaces; grouping/filtering begins after a choice.

Itinerary updates put Ram Raja Mandir first in a mixed editorial catalogue, clarify the guided fort visit, add breakfast/dinner guidance and packed-lunch offers, and use a short sanctuary outing after the fort on day 2. Both lengths include Chhatris sunset and Betwa View Dining at Orchha Resort, by the river. Arrival includes Chaturbhuj Temple followed by Ram Raja Mandir. The extra full day pairs morning rafting with pool time and the evening Sound & Light Show. Pool appears only on day 3 in the suggested three-night stay, after rafting: early evening in February–September, afternoon in October–January or without dates. Custom pool slots on other full days still use seasonal early-morning/afternoon suggestions. Date changes preserve selected outings; a conflicting pool slot becomes an unscheduled idea. Version-1 saved plans migrate without losing edits. Save as PDF opens the browser print/PDF options with a dedicated itinerary layout.

The suite uses the official click-loaded Instagram embed script with a persistent photograph while loading, timeout/retry and an independent original-reel link. Public playback was verified locally with the Worker Content Security Policy applied. The detailed specification below reflects these revisions.

Review baseline: 11 September 2026; plan finalised 12 September 2026. This document incorporates the live-site review, repository inspection, Instagram reference and the owner's decisions. It records the initial scope; implementation is local and the live website is unchanged.

## 1. Goal and design direction

Help guests choose a room, plan a worthwhile stay and reach live booking with their choices intact. Room bookings lead the site. Weddings and business events have separate journeys. Encourage longer visits through useful two-night and three-night itineraries while keeping one-night room bookings available.

Retain the hotel identity and real property photography. Use maroon for primary actions, sandstone as an accent, and light neutral surfaces with dark text. Retain the current serif/sans-serif pairing but reserve large serif type for short headings; use sentence-case, readable sans-serif labels. Use immersive photograph backdrops on the homepage and Weddings page; keep room choosers and utility pages compact with visible controls. Use real hotel imagery on the homepage; destination imagery belongs primarily in Explore Orchha.

Mobile foundations: 16px body and form text, at least 14px regular labels, 44px minimum interactive targets, 16–20px page gutters and a consistent spacing scale. Use responsive headline sizes, approximately 48px mobile section spacing and desktop columns where they aid comparison. Reserve the largest serif headlines for the immersive heroes.

Evidence: [audit and screenshots](</Users/gautamyadav/Documents/ChatGPT/Orchha Palace Hotel & Convention Centre/design/mobile-ux-audit.md>). The current mobile homepage is approximately 10,900px tall; rooms start beyond two screens. The event page has confirmed overflow at 360px. These are layout observations, not measured conversion losses.

## 2. Navigation, homepage and room booking

- Primary navigation: Rooms & suites, Weddings, Corporate events, Dining, Explore Orchha. Keep Hotel & amenities, Gallery, Contact & directions and policies in the secondary menu/footer. Label the corporate page itself “Meetings & Corporate Events.” Remove Offers from navigation while no active offers exist; preserve its URL with useful booking guidance.
- Homepage order: immersive palace photograph and introduction; date/guest controls below the hero; one image and an editorial rooms introduction; short hotel benefits with dining/wellness links; two-/three-night itinerary preview; separate Weddings and Corporate events entry cards; practical arrival/FAQ information; compact contact footer. Use the owner-selected palace exterior, a generous centered description and one View rooms + suites link. All five categories remain on the room chooser.
- Let the palace experience lead the first viewport and place date entry directly below it. Use one unobstructed bottom booking action when the inline action is outside the viewport. Suppress it during dialogs, menu use and keyboard entry. Reserve space so it never covers content.
- Make the privacy prompt compact and place it in reserved layout space. Show it only when an optional analytics integration is configured. Keep acceptance and refusal equally accessible; retain the user's choice and provide a footer preference control.
- On `/rooms/`, show the chooser immediately after a short heading. Filter by bed configuration and bathtub; retain all five existing room-detail URLs. Allow comparison of two rooms by size, beds, bathroom, aspect, inclusions and reservation method. On phones, display each comparison attribute followed by both room values, avoiding a wide table. Announce result counts and offer Clear filters for empty results.
- Room details begin with the name, useful facts, category gallery and booking action. Put lengthy amenity/inclusion lists into accessible disclosures. Preserve existing gallery swipe, previous/next, keyboard and full-screen functionality. Keep suite-specific call and WhatsApp actions.
- On the Presidential Suite detail/booking page, add “Tour the Presidential Suite” using the owner's [selected Instagram reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcQzGTNS48I/). Place it beside the photo gallery on desktop and immediately after the gallery on mobile, keeping the assisted reservation action easy to reach. Retain all existing suite photos. Use the official single-reel embed, loaded only after “Load video from Instagram” is selected; no autoplay. Initially show an existing suite photograph with a video label, without presenting it as a captured reel frame. Reserve space for the portrait video and embed controls without cropping either. Keep a visible “Watch on Instagram” link if the embed is unavailable, blocked or requires sign-in; viewing Instagram must never be necessary to enquire or book. Public playback has been verified locally with the deployment CSP applied; reconfirm it on the production domain before launch.
- Share one booking state between inline forms, the booking dialog and room pages, stored for the browsing session. Initially leave dates unselected; retain two adults and zero children. Remove the silent “two weeks from today” default. Show the selected dates, guest count and night count before handoff. Display field-specific errors and focus the first invalid field.
- Keep Maximojo and its verified parameter contract. Use “Check live rates” when a room code has not been verified; explain that the final room and bed configuration are selected or confirmed in the booking journey. Do not promise that a twin preference has been transmitted when it has not. Preserve only supported dates, occupancy, promo code and verified room code. Multi-room allocation remains in Maximojo. Clarify the engine's age bands and route unsupported infant/occupancy needs to reservations. Preserve the Presidential Suite's assisted-only booking.
- Give dining and wellness cards specific actions such as asking about a table at Annajal or a spa appointment, with the selected service included in the WhatsApp draft. Add category filters and the existing full-screen viewer to the property gallery. Replace public copy about internal approval processes with useful guest information.

Corporate trust carousel:

- Head it “Organisations we've hosted.” Include companies, public bodies and professional associations from the owner's confirmed event history, preserving all 12 positions in the [corporate logo register](</Users/gautamyadav/Documents/ChatGPT/Orchha Palace Hotel & Convention Centre/design/corporate-logo-register.md>). ## 4. Two- and three-night itinerary planner

Place the planner on the existing `/explore-orchha/` route. Offer **2 nights / 3 days** and **3 nights / 4 days**, defaulting to two nights. A short preview on the homepage leads directly into it. Do not add a one-night itinerary preset or a minimum-stay restriction to room search.

Guests first read a suggested stay, with visible two-/three-night choices, optional dates under Add travel dates, one chronological column and expandable days. The first day starts open; collapsed summaries name their experiences. Customise this stay reveals Change and a secondary More menu for Move/Remove, with Done returning to reading. The catalogue remains collapsed under Browse all 21 experiences and retains Heritage, Spiritual, Nature, Adventure, Rural & food, and At the hotel filters. Changing a filter changes the suggestions, not the guest's saved selections. All 17 supplied activity ideas belong in the catalogue, with booking status and source notes as specified in the [activity register](</Users/gautamyadav/Documents/ChatGPT/Orchha Palace Hotel & Convention Centre/design/itinerary-activity-register.md>).

Default editorial templates, subject to same-day access and service confirmation:

| Day | Two-night plan | Three-night plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | Check in after 2pm; Chaturbhuj Temple in the afternoon, then Ram Raja Mandir evening visit/aarti, with timing confirmation. | Same arrival, Chaturbhuj and evening temple visits. |
| Full day 1 | Guided fort visit in the morning; lunch and hotel downtime; Chhatris/Betwa sunset. Sound & Light Show is an evening alternative, not an automatically stacked commitment. | Guided fort visit; lunch and hotel downtime; Chhatris/Betwa sunset. |
| Full day 2 | — | Sanctuary outing after breakfast with a packed-lunch offer; hotel downtime; Sound & Light Show. Rural visits or confirmed river adventures remain alternatives. |
| Departure | Breakfast and check-out. Optional Chaturbhuj/VR visit only when onward travel allows, with luggage arrangements confirmed. | Breakfast and check-out; optional Laxmi Narayan/TaraGram visit when onward travel allows. |

- Arrival-day activities begin after the hotel's published 2pm check-in. The departure plan respects 10am check-out; it must not assume a room remains available afterwards. Show optional post-check-out activities separately.
- Use an optional early-morning pool block plus morning/afternoon/evening blocks and editorial duration estimates, not invented appointment times. Change opens a contextual bottom sheet on phones and a dialog on desktop, retaining the chosen day and time. Add or Move uses an explicit day/time choice; Remove keeps the experience in Saved ideas. Cancellation restores focus without scrolling to the catalogue. Already-scheduled experiences identify their source slot, and confirmation explains movement, displaced ideas and timing restrictions. Reserve meal/rest blocks. One substantial outing per half-day; offer replacement when a slot is occupied. Keep at most one fixed evening programme per day. Guided heritage walk is a guide option for the fort visit, not a duplicate second fort activity.
- Keep SavedItinerary version 2 and the existing storage key. Save only actual changes, distinguishing Suggested, Restored and Customised stays. Explicit two-/three-night links override restored length while retaining displaced ideas and providing Undo. One-step Undo lasts until the next change; Reset appears in Options only when the plan differs from its seasonal template. Storage failure leaves the planner usable and offers PDF/copy.
- Provide Save as PDF through the browser print/PDF options, Copy itinerary and “Ask the hotel to arrange this” through a guest-initiated WhatsApp draft. Share day order, selected activities and optional chosen dates. Adding an activity never makes a reservation. Clearly distinguish “View official information,” “Check permits,” and a genuinely verified operator booking link.
- Make Check rooms for this stay primary, Save as PDF secondary, and group Copy/WhatsApp beneath. Use an itinerary-specific mobile action showing selected length. Missing or outdated arrival dates are requested in context, proposing checkout after two or three nights. If valid existing room-search dates differ, let the guest explicitly keep or change them; never silently lengthen their booking. One-night and other durations remain valid in the ordinary booking form.
- Keep exact ticket prices, seasonal schedules and operator contacts in maintainable records with a source and review date. For v1, link to current prices/timings instead of hard-coding them into itinerary cards. River conditions, forest access, guides and rural visits require confirmation. Overnight camping stays available as an optional catalogue item, never a default activity overlapping a paid hotel night.
