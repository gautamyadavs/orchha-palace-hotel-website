## Owner revisions implemented 13 September 2026

The catalogue starts with Ram Raja Mandir, the fort complex and Chhatris/Betwa, followed by rafting and a mixed sequence of heritage, nature, river and rural experiences. This is editorial ordering for a useful first visit, not a measured popularity ranking. The public MP Tourism destination guide informed the emphasis; the order itself is a design judgement.

The default arrival afternoon includes Chaturbhuj Temple and the evening includes Ram Raja Mandir. The principal full day uses the guided fort visit and Chhatris sunset. Day 2 places a shorter sanctuary visit after the fort, with a lunch/rest pause and transfers subject to confirmation. Both stays end that evening with Chhatris sunset and Betwa View Dining at the sister property. The third night adds morning rafting, one pool/garden period and the Sound & Light evening. Guests see breakfast before activities on full days, breakfast before departure check-out, dinner after evening plans, and an offer to arrange packed lunch for sanctuary, birdwatching, rafting or trekking outings.

Pool is suggested only on day 3 of the three-night plan. After morning rafting, dated February–September visits use the cooler early evening before the show; October–January and undated visits use afternoon. Custom pool suggestions on other full days retain the early-morning/afternoon seasonal rule, subject to opening hours. This is the owner's scheduling preference, not a forecast or guarantee about weather. Existing saved choices are preserved during date changes. PDF export includes the current edited plan, meal notes, Saved ideas and hotel contact details; prices and reservations remain subject to confirmation.
