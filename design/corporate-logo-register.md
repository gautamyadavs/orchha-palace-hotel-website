IATO's official site supplies a lockup including the words “IATO Member”; it is reproduced as supplied under the hosted-organisations heading. No hotel membership claim is added. SBI uses the exact owner-provided source instead of the earlier unavailable official asset.

Omissions:

- **IRIA, rank 8:** excluded as the owner requested. The supplied domain returned unrelated content; there is no placeholder or public link to it.
- **ASISC U.P. & U.K., rank 11:** omitted because the regional website's artwork could not be reliably retrieved. No national CISCE substitute is used.

Capacity is established separately by venue and seating-layout data. The carousel does not infer event attendance, delegate numbers or equipment from logos. Recovered artwork can be inserted at its original rank after inspection, if the owner later requests it.
